About my academic path

I am an undergraduate student in Plant Breeding & Genetics at the University of Agriculture Faisalabad, Burewala sub-campus. My academic interests centre on crop genetic improvement, plant biotechnology, molecular biology and the responsible use of computational tools in biological research.

I am building an interdisciplinary foundation that connects conventional plant breeding with molecular methods, bioinformatics and data analysis. I am particularly interested in research that can contribute to resilient crops, sustainable production and practical benefits for farming communities.

03 / Selected work

Academic and computational projects

Computational project

PCSK9–Fexofenadine molecular docking study

A computational investigation exploring the interaction of fexofenadine with PCSK9, using the experimentally determined protein structure PDB: 6U2P as the structural reference.

  • Focus: protein–ligand preparation, docking workflow and interaction assessment.
  • Tools used in the project workflow include AutoDock Vina and PyMOL, with molecular-system preparation work involving GROMACS-related files.
Scope note: this is an in-silico project. Docking predictions do not establish biological efficacy or clinical activity. Molecular-dynamics completion and experimental validation are not claimed here.
Academic design project

Combined rice ideotype for Punjab, Pakistan

An academic ideotype-design project focused on the requirements of rice-growing environments in Punjab, with attention to Basmati production regions.

  • Integrates target plant architecture and crop-performance traits.
  • Compares candidate genes, favourable alleles, evidence and potential trade-offs using published research.
Scope note: a literature-informed breeding design, not a released variety or a field-validated genotype.
Literature / research concept

CRISPR/Cas9 strategy to improve oleic acid in canola

A review-oriented research concept examining targeted disruption of FAD2 genes in Brassica napus as a route to alter seed fatty-acid composition.

  • Focus: FAD2 function and its relationship with oleic-acid desaturation.
  • Considers gene-editing rationale, potential outcomes and biological trade-offs.
Scope note: literature-based study / proposed strategy; no gene-edited canola lines or experimental results are claimed.
